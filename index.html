Afshin (Sean) Sadeghi, PhD

AI researcher · representation learning, interpretability, AI safety

I am an AI researcher with a PhD in Artificial Intelligence, working at the intersection of representation learning, mechanistic interpretability, and AI safety. I study how large language models represent and use knowledge, and how their internal mechanisms can inform hallucination detection and model steering.

My work spans research on understanding LLM internals and on machine-learning representations for knowledge graphs. My goal is to improve how AI systems understand and reason about information, while making their behavior more interpretable and reliable.

Latest research

Two lines of work, each with an interactive page that walks through the paper.

Connections between LLM representations

Is the direction in a model’s hidden state that detects a concept the same one that changes the model’s behavior? And how much of this carries over from one model to another?

Graph inference for fact checking

Replacing opaque LLM-as-judge verdicts with explicit belief-function reasoning over retrieved knowledge-graph triples, so factuality checks are traceable and more reliable.
